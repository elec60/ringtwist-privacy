Ring Twist Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Ring Twist has no accounts, and the game itself plays fully offline. The app uses the network only for usage statistics and crash reports (with your consent where the law asks for it) and for one optional feature: getting more hints, by a reward video or, in the Cafe Bazaar edition, by a purchase.

Who is responsible

Mirhashem Mousavi Mehmandousti, the developer of Ring Twist, is the controller of your data in the sense of the GDPR. See "Contact" below.

Stored on your device

Your level progress, hint count, settings (language, theme) and your consent choices are stored locally on your device. They may be included in your personal device backup (Android backup, iCloud or a computer backup) if you have backups enabled. Deleting the app removes them.

Your consent choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland nothing described under "Usage statistics and crash reports" or "Reward videos" happens until you have agreed to it. When the app starts it asks Google whether consent is needed where you are, which transmits your IP address. The consent form appears the first time you choose to watch a reward video. You can open it at any time under Settings → "Privacy choices", to agree, refuse or withdraw; refusing is as easy as agreeing and costs you nothing in the game. Withdrawing does not affect what was processed before.

Elsewhere, the features below are active from the first launch where local law allows it.

Usage statistics and crash reports

The Google Play and App Store editions contain Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, so that problems can be found and fixed and the game improved. Both are switched off until you have agreed (or no consent is needed where you are). The Cafe Bazaar edition contains neither.

  • Analytics records how the game is played: levels started, won, failed, restarted or left, with the level number, moves, stars and time taken; hints used; and moves undone. This comes together with device model, system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and an app-instance identifier. It is used for advertising only if you also agreed to the advertising purposes.
  • Crashlytics records crashes: the stack trace, device model, system version, app version and an installation identifier. It is not used for advertising.

Both are provided by Google; see the Firebase privacy information.

Reward videos

There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. When you have run out of hints you can choose to watch a video for more; if you never tap that button, no ad is shown. Hints also come back by solving levels, so the video is never needed.

The videos are served by Google AdMob. To load and show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data such as your IP address (which gives an approximate location), the device's advertising ID, device and app information, and how you interact with an ad. On iOS the app never asks for permission to track you, so the advertising ID stays hidden from it there. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. See how Google uses this data.

Hint pack purchase (Cafe Bazaar edition)

The Cafe Bazaar edition has no videos; instead you can buy a pack of hints through Cafe Bazaar. Cafe Bazaar processes the payment; Ring Twist never sees your payment details and only learns that the pack was bought, so it can add the hints.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Usage statistics, crash reports and ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the rules on storing or reading information on your device).
  • Asking whether consent is needed, and remembering your answer: legal obligation and legitimate interest in meeting it (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
  • The hint pack purchase: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Recipients and transfers

Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC process the data above as described in their policies. Data may be transferred to the United States and other countries; Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for this.

How long data is kept

Analytics data tied to an identifier is kept for at most 14 months, crash reports for 90 days. Data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, please include how we can find your data when you write to us; deleting the app and resetting your advertising ID in the system settings cuts the link to past data.

Children

Ring Twist is not directed at children under 16, and no consent is asked of them knowingly.
